# Project context — MAIM publishing

Read this before any publishing task. It exists so this context is never re-explained.

**Major Dream Williams** in full.

## The design lock — overrides anything in this skill

`maim-integrity-publishing-design` is the authority on covers, marks and the author
photo. Read it before any visual work. In short:

- The supplied **MD-over-woven-hat** mark is the asset. Primary is the circular version,
  approved alternate is unencircled. **Never draw, trace, reinterpret or regenerate it.**
  No crown, crest, seal or substitute monogram — "crown" does not authorize a different
  symbol.
- The **supplied author photograph** is used unchanged apart from proportionate framing.
  **Never synthesize a replacement likeness.**
- Approved assets are hash-verified in
  `maim-integrity-publishing-design/references/approved-assets.json`. Recover a missing
  asset; never substitute a generated approximation.
- Verify with `scripts/verify_assets.py`, build with `scripts/build_covers.py`.

## The voice protocol — overrides anything in this skill

`MAJOR-AUTHOR-VOICE-PROTOCOL.md`: **AI may organize and edit, but may not invent
biography.** Every author section comes from a dated, consented recording. Author intakes
live in `knowledge/voice/`. Major approves each section before it reaches a reader.

Do not draft an author passage. Assemble from the transcripts or leave the slot empty.

## Standing cautions

- Both MAIM repositories are **public**. Never commit named individuals treated as sales
  prospects, private conversations, legal admissions or third-party allegations.
- Never publish a credential or a credential-bearing URL. Secrets live in environment
  variables.
- Do not call a preview print-ready. A physical spine needs final page count, printer
  trim, paper, binding, bleed and barcode specs.
- Invent no ISBNs, endorsements or review quotes.
