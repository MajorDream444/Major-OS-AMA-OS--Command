## Type

- **Display:** Oswald, Archivo Black, **Inter ExtraBold**, Anton, Bebas Neue,
  League Spartan, Cinzel
- **Body:** **Inter**, Manrope, Montserrat
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono

Inter is installed on this container and is on the canonical display list, so it
is used directly rather than substituted. Arial Narrow and Georgia are **not**
MAIM faces — they were an earlier mistake.
