# Alternate covers and release sequence

Status: production recommendation. The owner approved retaining both designs, not a launch date, storefront experiment or automatic rotation.

## Starting policy

Include both approved cover options in each digital edition purchase once the complete edition is ready. Keep one product/edition/format entitlement and record cover_id A/B in the manifest. Provide clearly labeled file choices with identical manuscript revisions. Do not create competing books, imply different content or charge twice solely for art.

Use one hero image and show the alternate in the gallery. Starting editorial suggestions: adult B, teen B, child A. These are not owner-selected winners. Both alternatives remain locked designs.

Consider the alternate hero after 8-12 weeks of actual traffic. Treat this as a proposed test window, not a proven optimal interval or automation. Keep the baseline if evidence is too thin. If testing, hold content, price and offer steady where practical; record cover/version/date/channel. Before/after results alone do not prove causation. Consider reader feedback and access outcomes alongside clicks and purchases.

Keep existing buyers' access. Avoid fake scarcity, discounts or new-edition claims when only the cover changes. A substantive manuscript revision needs its own version and consideration of edition/identifier implications.

## Sequence

Review L01 across all three editions, then continue L02-L10 without waiting for cover tests or final prices. A practical first pilot is the completed adult edition; teen and child release after their own editorial, educator/safeguarding, rights and accessibility reviews. This is a recommendation, not compulsory months of delay between ready editions. No scheduled dates are adopted.

Scope any standalone L01 mini-book explicitly as one lesson. Do not sell it as the complete edition ebook.

For print, keep both art directions but decide manufacturing and distribution feasibility before offering variants. Verify current external-platform and ISBN rules from official sources if that channel is selected; do not assume selectable cover support. Distinct physical inventory variants are considered only where real ordering/manufacturing requires them. Cover art alone does not authorize duplicate Stripe products.

Require complete approved files, precise inclusions, durable orders/entitlements, protected access, Resend delivery, retry/refund behavior and end-to-end test evidence. Design approval does not activate sales.
