## Editorial spine

The central lesson is the difference between knowing how to do something and building a process
another person can follow. Preserve Major's own morning-thought-to-Substack example:

1. Capture a morning thought.
2. Create a transcript.
3. Use NotebookLM to explore the sources through a generated discussion.
4. Return that transcript to agents to examine gaps and blind spots.
5. Shape the material into an article and choose supporting media.

The harder handoff decisions are audience trust, context, instinct, impact, and judgment. Preserve
Major's natural storytelling and do not replace it with generic instruction.
