Nine possible combinations exist: rabbit/rabbit, rabbit/elephant, rabbit/whale, elephant/rabbit, elephant/elephant, elephant/whale, whale/rabbit, whale/elephant, whale/whale. The inner size expresses relative scope, engineering and cost within its outer segment; it does not redefine the outer segment.

## Metadata and filing requirement
Every ingested item needs at least twelve distinct, meaningful controlled tags, plus an auditable record. Use facets such as ecosystem, workspace, entity, asset_type, topic, audience, workflow, lifecycle, sensitivity, rights, channel, language, offer, outer_segment, inner_size and owner_role. Do not fabricate a category to meet the count; flag an unclassified facet for review and retain the source. Tags should be namespace:value, stable, searchable and consistent across systems.

Record stable item ID, original filename, canonical path, source/provider, source timestamp, ingestion timestamp, SHA256 for byte assets, version, relationships, duplicate_of, rights evidence, classification evidence and last_verified_at. These metadata fields are not a substitute for meaningful tags. Original source is preserved. Exact duplicate removal requires byte/hash verification and a retained canonical copy; near-duplicates require review. Keep private contact/media manifests outside public GitHub.
