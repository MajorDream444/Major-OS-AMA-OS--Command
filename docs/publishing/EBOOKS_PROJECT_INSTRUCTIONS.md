`MAJOR-AUTHOR-VOICE-PROTOCOL.md` in `MajorDream444/MAIM-LUCKY-LIBRARY` governs every
collection. Its binding line:

> **AI may organize and edit, but may not invent biography.**

Any passage in Major's first person — a memory, a place he has been, a person he knew,
something that happened to him, what he felt — must trace to a recording, transcript or
dictation. If you cannot cite the source, **you may not write it**. Leave an `[INTAKE]`

**A Word from Major** (300–700 words) · **Major's Field Note** (a lived anecdote) ·
**The Question I Still Carry** · anything biographical · any claim about what he believes,
felt or decided

from edited narration. If a memory is uncertain, label it for verification. Historical
claims inside anecdotes need sources.
