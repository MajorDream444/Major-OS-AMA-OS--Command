## 01 · The night I found out I was the bottleneck

**One idea:** busy is not load-bearing. Busy means your day is full.
Load-bearing means it falls down when you step away.

**Open with the dinner party.** Eighteen people, ran out of wine, the trip for
the cheaper bottles. The real failure was pride, not planning.

**Leave in:** that it is not solved. The three reasons people stay — fear of
success, control earned by disappointment, atrophy.

**Assignment:** the Dependency Score. Ten things only you could do, scored 1–3.

## 02 · What lives in your head is not expertise

**One idea:** undocumented knowledge is risk, not mastery. It cannot be
improved, delegated, sold or inherited.

**The spine is your own line:** *when you want to automate something, you have
to itemise everything that you do.*

**Leave in:** that step four is usually "and then I look at it and I know" —
and that itemising is what exposes it.

**Assignment:** the System Audit. One repeated task, written as steps, with
every improvisation marked.

## 03 · Your ceiling is a number, and you can calculate it

**One idea:** the arithmetic, done out loud. Real hours, real weeks, real rate.
Most people have never done this because the number is unpleasant and vague
feels better.

**Leave in:** three ways past it — raise the rate, add people, stop selling
hours — and that only the third changes the shape.

**Assignment:** the Time Ceiling calculator. The number, the goal, the gap, and
which of the three routes you are actually on versus the one you assume.

This is the most useful video of the five. If one is going to get watched to
the end by someone with the problem, it is this one.

## 04 · Every response correct, and the boat still sinks

**One idea:** at no stage is the founder doing anything wrong. Every single
response is correct. The boat still sinks.

**The wooden boat.** Termites ate the wood, every hole gets plugged, it is just
you on the boat. *You see a problem, you're on it. You see a problem, you're on
it.* And nothing ever gets fixed.

**Leave in:** *most people think they own a business, but you actually own a
job that you're shackled to.* And where the instinct comes from — sport. Run
harder, jump higher, versus measure three times and cut once.

**Close on the 80/20 restaurant.** The fine dining room, and everyone actually
comes at lunch. *Nobody cares what you see. I want rice and peas and curry
chicken at lunchtime.*

**Assignment:** Replace Yourself. Lowest Dependency Score, brief written, handed
over this week.

## 05 · Michael Jordan won. Also, the triangle offense.

**One idea:** *your system is what saves you. Period, point blank.*

A recipe is a system — one ounce of this, two teaspoons of that, same result
every time. Four things a system gives you that effort never will:
predictability, scale, data, longevity.

**Leave in the honest limit,** which is why this one is last: systematise your
hands and you get free; systematise your **thinking** and you stop thinking.
*AI changes your need for awareness. It can make you not be aware.*

**End on the question you actually ask:** what blind spots are we not looking
at? What am I missing?

**Assignment:** build one AI employee for a single task off the Dependency
Score. Watch it run end to end before trusting it.
