## Channel description

Paste into **YouTube Studio → Customisation → Basic info → Description**.
Limit is 1,000 characters. This is 712, leaving room to add links later.

```
If you stopped working for two weeks, would your business still bring in clients?

Most founders think they have a time problem. They buy planners, wake earlier, get
ruthless with the calendar — and none of it holds, because it was never a time
problem. It's a structure problem.

MAIM Academy is for people who already know their work and want it to stop
depending on them. Coaches, consultants, creators, practitioners. Caribbean
diaspora and anyone who sells what they know.

Short, practical videos on building systems that run without you — and using AI
to do it. If you can text, you can use AI.

I'm Major Dream Williams. South Bronx, Mount Vernon, Jamaican family, twenty years
across finance, crypto and AI. I'm not teaching this from the far side. I'm still
in it.

Fix the system. Fix the income.

Free guide and the founding cohort list → majoraimindset.com
```

### Why it is written this way

- **Opens with the question**, which is already the Instagram bio and the book's
  core question. Three places, one sentence.
- **Names who it is for** in the first third. YouTube search and the viewer both
  need that early.
- Carries the keywords a search will actually use — systems, AI, consultants,
  coaches, Caribbean — without a keyword list.
- **"I'm still in it"** is from the manuscript and is the line that makes the
  rest credible.
- **One CTA**, the honest one. Enrollment is closed, so it points at the guide
  and the waitlist, never a checkout.

## Banner

`build/banner/maim-academy-banner-2560x1440.png`

Upload at **Customisation → Branding → Banner image**. 2560×1440, under 6 MB.

Every legible element sits inside the **1235×338 safe area**, which is all a
phone shows. The Constricted Flow field runs the full width for TV and desktop,
and the type sits in the clearing the waist opens by itself.

It carries the same CTA as the description: free guide, cohort list, the URL.
A banner that only decorates is a wasted surface.

only. **Do not upload that one.**
