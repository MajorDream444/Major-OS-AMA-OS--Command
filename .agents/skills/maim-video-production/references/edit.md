# Edit and delivery

## Google Vids

The edit bay. Assembling footage, title cards, b-roll, captions and transitions
is what it is for.

**Its AI avatar and synthetic voiceover are out.** So is every equivalent
feature in HeyGen, Higgsfield or any other tool in this stack. The presenter is
Major, filmed. This is not a style preference — it is the synthesised-likeness
lock, and it is the one thing that would undercut a channel built on being
straight with people.

Cutting shorts by hand in Vids is **free**, which matters: see the credit
arithmetic below.

## Chain, per video

1. Upload **unlisted** to `@MAIM_ACADEMY` (`UCAxy4YB0VlQgrmQiKowu04g`).
   `channelId` is the only reliable identifier — vidIQ has served a stale handle
   for a full day.
2. `vidiq_video_transcript` → **read it.** Garbage captions mean the audio.
3. `vidiq_score_title` before committing to a title.
4. Thumbnail via `maim-brand-production` — 1280 × 720, Constricted Flow
   language, same tokens as the book covers so channel and book read as one.
5. Clips — budget first, see below.
6. Schedule through Metricool only after confirming the destination account.
   Metricool's Instagram is **`amasolagi`**, not `@maim_academy`.

## Credit arithmetic

150 vidIQ credits a month, resetting **23 November**.

| Call | Cost |
|---|---|
| `vidiq_generate_clips` | **9 credits per minute of source** |
| `vidiq_video_transcript` | ~5 |
| `vidiq_user_videos` | ~5 |
| `vidiq_ig_profile` | 5 |

A 12-minute video clipped through vidIQ costs ~108 — most of a month. Five
videos would cost ~500, more than three months of budget.

**So: clip one video through vidIQ, cut the rest by hand in Vids.** Or clip a
trimmed section rather than a whole video. Check `vidiq_balance` before
spending.

## Upload cap

The daily cap counts **uploads, not minutes**. A 12-minute video and a
20-second clip cost the same one slot.

**Long-form first.** A full day's cap was once spent on nine short clips while
the main recordings waited — that is the mistake this line exists to prevent.

## Staging versus publishing

**Unlisted is a staging state, not a publishing decision.** Unlisted makes
footage readable by vidIQ and clears the ~3 MB ceiling on Drive downloads
through the connector. It does not mean anyone decided to release it. Nothing
goes public without the owner saying so.
